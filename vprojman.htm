Project Management:

Validien consultants are experienced in all major project management methodologies such as PMBOK, Classic System Design Life Cycle, Agile / Scrum, Scaled Agile, and Test Driven Development. We use Jira, Workfront, Kanban, Confluence, Azure DevOps, and ITIL Service Management models.

We understand the role of Project Management when it comes to contracting our services. Your outcomes become our goal. We produce project deliverables according to defined frameworks, or customized to your own management needs. Validien excels in the Planning, Executing, and Controlling practice areas.

We can produce or maintain Project Charters, Communication Plans, Requirement Breakdowns, Product Backlogs, Release and Change Management, and Project boards. We develop KPI's, Value-Earned, and Gantt, Sankey, and Burndown charts where these become useful.
