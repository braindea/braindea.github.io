Project Management

Project management that keeps delivery aligned, visible, and under control.

Validien supports projects with practical planning, execution, tracking, and governance across traditional, agile, and hybrid delivery environments.

Contact UsBack to Our Business

Delivery support built around outcomes

Validien consultants are experienced with major project management approaches including PMBOK, classic SDLC methods, Agile, Scrum, Scaled Agile, and test-driven development environments. We work with the delivery model that best fits the project rather than forcing every engagement into the same framework.

Project management is most effective when it connects planning, communication, execution, and control in a way that supports the actual outcome the client needs.

If your organization needs stronger project controls, better communication artifacts, clearer backlog management, or more disciplined execution across technical initiatives, Validien can provide practical project management support tailored to the engagement.

Project management support can include

  • Project charters, communication plans, and delivery frameworks.
  • Requirements breakdowns, product backlogs, and release planning.
  • Change management, project boards, and workstream coordination.
  • KPI tracking, earned value analysis, and project status reporting.
  • Burndown, Gantt, Sankey, and other project visualizations where useful.
  • Tool support across Jira, Workfront, Kanban boards, Confluence, Azure DevOps, and ITIL-oriented service models.

Methodologies

PMBOK, SDLC, Agile, Scrum, Scaled Agile, hybrid delivery, and structured governance approaches.

Common tools

Jira, Workfront, Kanban, Confluence, Azure DevOps, and ITIL-aligned service workflows.

Related services

Project management often overlaps with business consulting, architecture, integration, cloud delivery, and reporting support.
